# PalsChat user guide

## Getting started
1. Open https://palschat.onrender.com. The first load may take up to a minute.
2. Choose **Create account**, enter your name, pick **Male**, **Female** or **Prefer not to say**, then your email and a password of at least 8 characters.
3. Your gender only decides your default picture, and it can be set once.

## Chatting
- **Chats** tab: your conversations. **People** tab: everyone you can message. Tap a person to start.
- Press **Enter** to send, **Shift+Enter** for a new line.
- Ticks: one is sent, two are delivered, blue is read.
- **Reply, delete:** open the small arrow on a message. *Delete for me* hides it for you; *Delete for everyone* works for your own messages within 48 hours.
- **Pictures and files:** use the paperclip (up to 4 MB). Allowed: pictures, PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text and CSV.
- **Jump to latest:** when you scroll up, a button appears to take you to the newest message.

## Groups
Use the group button in the chat list to name a group and add people. Open the group's menu to see members, rename it, add or remove people (if you are an admin) or leave.

## Managing a chat
Open the chat's menu to **pin** (up to 3), **mute**, **archive**, **block**, **clear** (empties it for you only) or **delete** it (for you only). Archived chats are under **Archived** in the chat list.

## Settings (gear icon)
- **Appearance:** Light, Dark or System, and an accent colour.
- **Notifications:** message sounds and desktop pop-ups. Muted chats never alert. If the browser blocks pop-ups, allow them in the site settings.
- **Account:** edit your picture, name and about line; change your password; see and end your signed-in devices; sign out.

## Privacy and safety
- **Block** someone to stop messages in both directions.
- **Password and devices** shows every device signed in. End any you do not recognise.
- **Delete my account** (in Password and devices) erases your details. Your old messages stay in other people's chats, shown as "Deleted user".
- Messages are encrypted while travelling to the server, but the system is not end-to-end encrypted.
